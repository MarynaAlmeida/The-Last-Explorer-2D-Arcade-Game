\vspace{1em}

\item \textbf{Communication:}
      \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item Communications will be done through the platform called Discord.
      \end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Meetings:}
      \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item A mandatory meeting will be held on Thursdays at 4:00--4:30\,pm PST.
      \item An optional meeting will be held on Mondays at 1:00--1:30\,pm PST.
      \item An optional meeting will be held on Wednesdays at 1:00--1:30\,pm PST.
      \end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Task Management:}
      \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item The work will be gathered in its totality and split up evenly among team members.
      \item Members can work individually and can notify members if they encounter any issues.
      \end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Ensuring Fair Work Distribution:}
      \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item The GitHub repository’s graph will be checked regarding contributions to any branches.
      \item Team members will collectively decide on what work to allow an even distribution.
      \end{itemize}

\item \textbf{The Internal Deadlines:}
      \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item The plan is to allow at least a few days before the deadline for work to be completed.
      \item The Thursday meeting will be a chance to check on team members’ current work and discussion of internal deadlines.
      \end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Issues of a Group Members Contributions:}
      \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item The team member will be given a warning if the rest of the group decides there is a lack of contribution.
      \item If this continues, the work will be distributed to the other members.
      \item This can lead to a grade down on the team members grade during peer evaluations.
      \end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Issues of Missing an Internal/Final Deadline:}
      \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item A warning could be provided, and they may be reimbursed an additional day after the deadline.
      \item This can lead to a grade down on the team members grade during peer evaluations.
      \item This can be avoided through a reasonable excuse with proof for missing the deadline and the rest of the group will decide if the grade down consequence is necessary.
      \end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}
